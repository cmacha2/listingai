## Understanding eBay's Search Algorithm (Cassini)

eBay's search algorithm, known as Cassini, considers multiple factors when ranking listings:

### Primary Ranking Factors
1. **Relevance**: How well your listing matches the search query
2. **Performance**: Your seller metrics and listing engagement
3. **Buyer Experience**: Customer satisfaction and listing quality

### Key Performance Metrics
- **Defect Rate**: Keep below 5% for optimal ranking
- **Late Shipment Rate**: Maintain under 3%
- **Cases Opened**: Minimize disputes and returns
- **Detailed Seller Ratings (DSRs)**: Aim for 4.8+ stars

## Keyword Research for eBay Success

Effective keyword research is the foundation of eBay SEO. Here's how to find the right keywords:

### 1. Use eBay's Search Suggestions

Start typing your main keyword in eBay's search bar and note the auto-suggestions. These are real searches from eBay users.

**Example Process:**
1. Type "bluetooth headphones"
2. Note suggestions like "bluetooth headphones wireless"
3. Record variations and long-tail keywords

### 2. Analyze Competitor Listings

Study successful competitors to identify effective keywords:

- **Top-selling listings**: What keywords do they use?
- **Title structure**: How do they organize keywords?
- **Category placement**: Which categories perform best?

### 3. Use Keyword Research Tools

**Free Tools:**
- **eBay Search Bar**: Auto-suggestions
- **Google Keyword Planner**: Search volume data
- **Ubersuggest**: Keyword ideas and difficulty

**Paid Tools:**
- **Terapeak**: eBay-specific market research
- **SEMrush**: Comprehensive keyword analysis
- **Ahrefs**: Competitor keyword research

## Title Optimization Strategies

Your title is the most important SEO element. eBay allows 80 characters, so use them wisely.

### Title Structure Formula

```
[Brand] [Product Type] [Key Features] [Model/Size] [Condition]
```

**Example:**
❌ Poor: "Nice headphones for sale"
✅ Good: "Sony WH-1000XM4 Wireless Noise Canceling Headphones Bluetooth Black New"

### Title Best Practices

1. **Front-load important keywords**: Place most important terms first
2. **Include brand names**: High search volume and trust factor
3. **Add specific details**: Model numbers, sizes, colors
4. **Use relevant adjectives**: New, vintage, premium, professional
5. **Avoid keyword stuffing**: Keep it natural and readable

### Common Title Mistakes

- **Excessive punctuation**: !!!! LOOK HERE !!!!
- **Irrelevant keywords**: Adding popular but unrelated terms
- **ALL CAPS**: Looks spammy and unprofessional
- **Repetitive words**: Bluetooth wireless bluetooth headphones
- **Filler words**: "WOW", "AMAZING", "L@@K"

## Category Selection and Item Specifics

Choosing the right category and filling out item specifics improves visibility and relevance.

### Category Best Practices

1. **Choose the most specific category**: Don't use broad categories
2. **Research competitor categories**: See where similar items rank well
3. **Consider secondary categories**: For items that fit multiple categories
4. **Use eBay's category suggestions**: They know their algorithm best

### Item Specifics Optimization

Item specifics are crucial for:
- **Filtered search results**: Buyers use filters frequently
- **Mobile optimization**: Easier browsing on mobile devices
- **Algorithm signals**: Helps eBay understand your product

**Complete These Fields:**
- Brand
- Model
- Condition
- Size/Dimensions
- Color
- Material
- Features
- Compatibility

## Description Optimization

While titles get you found, descriptions convert browsers into buyers.

### Description Structure

1. **Opening hook**: Grab attention immediately
2. **Key benefits**: What problems does it solve?
3. **Detailed specifications**: Technical details
4. **Usage scenarios**: How/when to use the product
5. **Trust signals**: Warranty, return policy, seller credentials

### SEO Elements in Descriptions

- **Keyword integration**: Natural placement of search terms
- **Bullet points**: Easy scanning for mobile users
- **HTML formatting**: Bold important features
- **Cross-selling**: Mention compatible products
- **Local keywords**: If targeting specific regions

## Image Optimization for SEO

Images don't just sell products—they impact search rankings too.

### Image SEO Factors

1. **Main image quality**: Clear, professional, white background
2. **Image quantity**: Use all 12 available slots
3. **Alt text optimization**: eBay uses image file names
4. **Loading speed**: Optimized file sizes for fast loading

### Image Best Practices

- **File naming**: Use descriptive names with keywords
- **Image size**: Minimum 500x500 pixels, recommended 1000x1000
- **File format**: JPG for photos, PNG for graphics
- **Compression**: Balance quality with file size

## Pricing Strategy and SEO

Your pricing strategy affects search ranking and conversion rates.

### Pricing Factors

1. **Competitive pricing**: Research similar listings
2. **Buy It Now vs. Auction**: BIN often ranks higher
3. **Free shipping**: Include shipping in item price
4. **Best Offer**: Increases engagement signals

### Pricing Tools

- **eBay Sold Listings**: Historical pricing data
- **Terapeak**: Market analysis and pricing trends
- **Price comparison sites**: Cross-platform research

## Advanced SEO Techniques

### 1. Seasonal Optimization

Adjust keywords and titles for seasonal trends:
- **Holiday keywords**: "Christmas gift", "Valentine's Day"
- **Seasonal terms**: "Summer", "Back to school"
- **Event-based**: "Super Bowl", "Black Friday"

### 2. Long-Tail Keyword Strategy

Target specific, lower-competition phrases:
- **Generic**: "bluetooth headphones" (high competition)
- **Long-tail**: "sony noise canceling headphones for travel" (lower competition)

### 3. Cross-Platform Optimization

Optimize for buyers coming from:
- **Google searches**: SEO-friendly titles work on Google too
- **Social media**: Shareable, attractive listings
- **Mobile apps**: Mobile-optimized descriptions

### 4. A/B Testing

Test different approaches:
- **Title variations**: Test different keyword arrangements
- **Price points**: Find the optimal price for ranking and conversion
- **Image sets**: Different main images for better CTR

4. **Search ranking**: Position for target keywords
5. **Traffic sources**: Where your visitors come from

### Tools for Tracking

- **eBay Seller Hub**: Built-in analytics

## Common SEO Mistakes to Avoid

### 1. Keyword Stuffing
Overusing keywords makes titles unreadable and can hurt rankings.

### 2. Ignoring Mobile Users
Over 60% of eBay traffic comes from mobile devices.

### 3. Poor Image Quality
Blurry or unprofessional images hurt both SEO and conversions.

### 4. Incomplete Item Specifics
Missing details reduce visibility in filtered searches.

### 5. Neglecting Customer Service
Poor seller metrics directly impact search rankings.
